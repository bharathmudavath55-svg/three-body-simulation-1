☀ Trisolar — Three-Body Simulator

An interactive 3D gravitational N-body physics simulator with periodic orbits, real-time trajectory visualization, numerical integrators, and parameter sharing.

Controls & Interaction

  • Space — Play / Pause simulation
  • R — Reset current simulation
  • Left-click drag — Orbit camera in 3D
  • Right-click drag — Pan camera view
  • Scroll wheel — Zoom in / out
  • Click on sphere — Select body and highlight properties
  • H — Toggle this help modal
